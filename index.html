Done-For-You Service Addendum

90-Day 3× Qualified Meetings Guarantee

Our 3× guarantee applies to all Done-For-You engagements and is written directly into your kickoff agreement so expectations are measurable from day one.

1. Week-one baseline
During strategy onboarding, we audit your trailing 90-day average of qualified sales meetings and document your agreed monthly baseline.
2. Mutual qualification standard
Together we define ICP filters (company size, geography, decision-maker role, and attendance criteria) so only genuine pipeline opportunities count.
3. 90-day launch window
The 90-day clock starts when both outbound sequences and inbound campaigns launch together (typically around week two).
4. Zero-cost continuation remedy
If qualified meetings booked over the 90-day window fall below 3× your baseline, OneReach Human Experts keep running your campaigns at $0 service fee until the target is met. Client-owned ad spend and third-party media budgets remain pass-through.

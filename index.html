The 90-day clock starts when both outbound sequences and inbound campaigns launch together (typically around week two).

If qualified meetings booked over the 90-day window fall below 3× your baseline, OneReach Human Experts keep running your campaigns at $0 service fee until the target is met. Client-owned ad spend and third-party media budgets remain pass-through.
